Does the ranking work?

Every month the brain ranks all stocks. The test: did its top 20% beat its bottom 20% over the next month? The line adds up that gap month after month. A line rising steadily means the ranking works. Flat or falling means it doesn't. The momentum line is the classic simple rule (buy last year's winners) for comparison.
