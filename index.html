How does this work?

🏪1. A company makes things.

Phones, shoes, games, food. People buy them.

🍕2. You can own a tiny slice.

A slice of a company is called a share. Anyone can buy one.

⚖️3. The price moves every day.

Many people want a slice: the price goes up. Few people want it: the price goes down.

💶4. That is how money is made.

An example: buy a slice for €10. Sell it later for €12. You made €2. Sell it for €8 and you lost €2.

🎲5. Nobody knows what comes next.

This page looks at the past and shows the chance. It is a guess, never a promise.
